## Arithmetic and authority

A trusted server policy pins existing full-admin operators, authority reference/proof, validity and a finite versioned recipe catalog. Recipes declare a procedure and explicitly labelled variant, source/business authority, validity, up to32 distinct fee lines and bounded additional30-day blocks. Every recipe accounts for service, government, bank and partner components; a component may have several lines. The browser cannot supply amounts, evidence, omit a required component or change the recipe after preview. Manual category/variant and block-count inputs are not inferred from qualification/profile facts and do not prove legal eligibility.

Only canonical bounded decimal strings in USD/KGS are accepted. Decimal context is explicit. A line uses base plus an explicitly chosen number of additional blocks, and an evidenced urgency factor may multiply only a government line. Service/bank/partner lines never inherit that factor. Unknown urgency remains unknown. Fractional-cent results require a separate rounding policy and block a complete result; no rounding, rate conversion, estimated bank2.9%, discount, refund or payer rule is invented.

Known zero, unknown and not-applicable are separate. Zero/NA require source evidence and a reason within the selected recipe scope; unknown may have no known currency. Missing components invalidate the recipe. Any unknown/unavailable/expired/overflow/sub-cent item blocks complete totals, leaving labelled known subtotals by currency/component. All-exempt recipes do not invent a currency or price. A full internal arithmetic result still does not determine FX or settlement amount on payment day.

## Scope, preview and revisions

One quote targets one exact case/application/applicant/attempt; person/application quantity is always one. Group invoice aggregation is outside this slice. The source receipt pins immutable application/applicant identity hashes and the exact observed application event ID, revision, hash and status. Later facts do not rewrite historical calculations. Profile, passport, client text and payer/contact facts are not consumed for tariff selection. Terminal approved/refused/closed or superseded attempts cannot receive a fresh quote. A successor application has a separate history.

Preview is read-only. Save takes the exact signed recipe/policy, parameters, target, quote revision, calculator and preview hash, then locks case→application and recomputes before appending. A changed source, policy, date, amount or revision requires a new preview. Policy is rechecked against the timestamp actually persisted; backwards clocks are rejected. No application fact, contract amount, payment or refund is written.

Additive migration0030 adds only `entry_quote_revisions`: scoped composite foreign keys, predecessor/revision/request uniqueness and immutable ORM/bulk guards. Every row stores the command, complete original policy/recipe snapshot, source pin, calculator version, authority snapshot and exact result. Reads replay all revisions and compare semantic results, not just hashes. A retained calculator registry preserves old arithmetic; changing the active policy/catalog does not make old receipts unreadable. Hashes are integrity checks, not external authenticity against a database administrator replacing every trusted anchor with another internally valid history.

Current identity revocation is checked before DB/retry access. Exact authorized request retries carry the original calculator version and return the original receipt before current policy/CAS/limit checks. Each retained calculator owns a literal output version independent of the active version; new previews resolve the active version at call time. A separately recomputed current overlay marks superseded, changed-source, changed-calculator, incomplete, expired or withdrawn-policy quotes unusable. An old receipt retry cannot reactivate present authority. History uses canonical type-sensitive equality, rejects bool/int substitution, and requires the observed application event revision never to rewind between quote revisions. History is bounded to128 revisions,1MiB per event and8MiB total, with the same limits during replay. Unknown commit retries preserve the entire original signed envelope even if rereading storage also fails.
