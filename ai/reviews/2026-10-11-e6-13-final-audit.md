Replace only the second quote's source pin with the real event 1 and recompute its preview, request and snapshot hashes. Policy, recipe, amounts, application identity and application event journal remain untouched. Require at least nondecreasing observed event revision across quote history. Do not replace exact pins with timestamp-prefix reconstruction; later/backdated source growth must remain readable.

This is an impossible-history semantic consistency gap, not a claim of cryptographic authenticity against a database administrator. The reproduction does not replace trusted policy anchors or the authoritative application history.
