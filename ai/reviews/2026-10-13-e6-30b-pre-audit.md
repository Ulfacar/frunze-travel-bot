# E6-30B — independent pre-audit: specialist decision → internal quote

2026-10-13. Reviewer: `publication_review`. **CRITICAL**, reviewed-local target, no release. This is a design pre-audit, not implementation approval. No runtime/tests/config edits, tests, PostgreSQL, provider calls, `.env`, live records, source approvals or E630A recertification.

**Recommendation:** one bounded, immutable, explicitly delegated specialist-decision journal, plus an atomic link to the existing internal quote journal. Use a source-indexed capability catalogue for the different SOP outcomes; do not build fourteen automatic qualification engines. Start the connected positive slice with the exact India/business/10-day case and keep unsupported outcomes as explicit referrals. All real authority defaults remain empty. This work alone does not close J01/J02.

## Source and current implementation

Assessment uses actual PDF-derived §4, §19.1–19.14, Б.8, fixed J01/J02 definitions and current code. Baseline HEAD: `7b45f6c56c22adef80a3f4e79aa1aae8d7a3fe0d`. All 25 scoped files in `runs/e630b-pre-audit/source-identities.json` equal HEAD after LF normalization. Manifest raw SHA256: `d9afecec32ddc65a7956823796dbe07432f5fe65698f7b79d62c571daacb25da`. It also records relevant full-archive page hashes. Source PDF identity: `141b9e1e570c916aab65f3dc40e927cc61ef182e1d155b997a5123091e36cca2`. E630A and unrelated UI were not reviewed anew.

Important findings:

1. **E628 is connected already.** `connected_qualification.assess` reconciles personal profile, personal history, interview/ACK and active source under locks. Its positive scope is single ordinary-passport tourism abroad, NO_VISA, with whole-trip and registration checks. It is read-only and keeps `may_quote=False`. Do not replace this with the obsolete E615 “interview→qualification absent” claim.
2. **The source publication does not contain a B product approval.** The 208 typed units contain regimes, deadlines, fees, registration, parameters, stops and templates; inspection found no visa-product qualification kind and no §19 units. `KG.VR.GENERAL` describes entry procedure, and `KG.FEE.SERVICE.B` a service fee. Neither establishes §19.3/§4.3 host/purpose suitability. The complete 94-page archive preserves those sections, but its hashes are not approval. `complete_documents_v1_1` contains product/checklist clauses, not legal classification authority.
3. **Existing quotes are deliberately arithmetic only.** `entry_quotes._scope`, `_pin`, `_preview_data`, `_history` and `_overlay` bind case/application/person and the application event. A recipe is matched only to `application.procedure`, not to TS/B/FF etc. Thus any visa recipe can currently be calculated against a visa application; that is correct for the existing manual arithmetic tool but insufficient for a decision-bound quotation.
4. **Existing source/role policies are not interchangeable delegations.** Publisher permission authorizes publication; interview permission authorizes collection; package permission authorizes metadata acceptance; process permission authorizes specific transitions/attestations; quote permission authorizes calculation. None automatically delegates a new substantive specialist decision.
5. **The existing interview bridge must not be broadened silently.** `assessment_interview.reconcile` rejects business/material branches, unresolved stops and handoffs whose reason is not `intake_complete`. This is appropriate for its NO_VISA scope. A specialist needs a separate explicit reconciliation of an acknowledged risk handoff, not removal of those guards.

## Observable result and explicit non-results

The operator sees the exact person, current personal facts, pending requirements, source authority and approved decision scope; records a dated specialist conclusion for **internal planning**; then previews/saves a permitted existing quote recipe for the same case/person/application. A saved quote shows which decision and facts it used and whether that link is still current.

Keep independent statuses: recorded specialist conclusion; current internal-calculation permission; arithmetic complete/incomplete; current source availability. Do not collapse them into `eligible=True` or overwrite E628's `may_quote`. No client offer, sending, legal guarantee, visa issuance, work permission, stay extension, package acceptance, invoice, signature, payment or contract-price change occurs. Existing channel stops/ACK/consent holds remain in force.

An internal estimate may precede document collection (§19.1). Missing invitation scans or an unaccepted package can be explicit pending prerequisites; do not force fake package approval to calculate a cost. Conversely, a conditional estimate must not be described as complete qualification. Actual contradictory purpose, missing authority or an unsupported route cannot be hidden in generic “documents pending”.

## Smallest policy and record contract

### Server-owned specialist policy and capability catalogue

Use a separate immutable, validated, empty-by-default policy. Its minimum content is a version/algorithm, reference/proof, validity, current delegated operator logins, source identities and a closed list of permitted capability IDs. Current authorization requires the existing full-admin gate/current configured identity **and** this delegation; do not expand to ordinary managers implicitly. Restrict scope per capability, not merely `can_review=True` for all products. Retain the exact policy snapshot/hash with each decision.

Each capability should bind:

- Exact original source SHA, full archive content identity and relevant page/text/block hashes; explicit review reference/proof for the identified §4/§19 clauses, not an arbitrary typed section string. Pin relevant document/process/condition catalogues by hash where consumed. A deterministic source index is sufficient; importing all paragraphs as invented executable legal rules is unnecessary.
- Allowed outcome, product and procedure (and any mode such as new/extension/change/consultation), material input fields, permitted unresolved prerequisites and required typed specialist findings/evidence. Scope a decision to one applicant. Represent dependencies on another principal/employer with explicit evidence; never inherit their applicant identity, passport or stay history.
- Required existing publication-unit IDs/hashes and current checks where applicable. Reuse the current publication gate; a new policy must not override withdrawn, conflicting, unverified or unavailable units. If relevant SOP clauses are outside that unit system, their separate explicit source-review approval is required; a PDF/archive hash alone is insufficient. Keep preserved `[VERIFY]`, `[DECISION]` and conflict flags unresolved unless an explicit approved decision resolves that exact item.
- Exact allowed quote policy hash and recipe ID/hash(s), keyed to the selected product/procedure. Do not authorize every `visa` recipe for B merely because the procedure matches. Urgency, additional blocks, currencies and unknown components retain their existing arithmetic semantics. An internal zero/exempt component is not a zero-value signed contract.

The catalogue can list all SOP outcomes without enabling all of them in a deployed policy. An enum label is not implemented or approved behavior. A materially applicable field that has no supported mapping must produce an explicit unresolved result, not be silently ignored. Do not turn this into a browser-supplied arbitrary rule language.

### Decision record

Recommended closed dispositions are the equivalents of **needs specialist evidence**, **declined/not serviceable**, **recorded manual route for internal calculation**, and **withdrawn**. Exact names are implementation choices. A NO_VISA/manual referral record need not fabricate a visa application solely to satisfy the quote API; quoting requires a real application in one of the existing six supported procedures. Tour/hotel upsells remain outside this KG quote model.

Retain a versioned command and immutable provenance containing:

- Case/applicant and optional exact application/attempt/procedure; request key, expected decision revision, actor, decision time and original result identity. Use strict scalar/date/enum types and bounded references, lists and text.
- Latest profile ID/revision/hash/source; opaque passport continuity identity and current personal history ID/head/hash (or explicit absence); interview header ID/hash, original profile pin, current event ID/revision/hash, current language/catalogue/policy, handoff-card hash and ACK. Application head/hash and successor state are required for a quote-target decision.
- Decision policy/capability/source-index identities; active publication version/source/bundle/projection/condition catalogue/manifest/revision; the relevant unit-review evidence and observed cutoff. Record original authority identities and timestamps sufficiently to validate a historical receipt without consulting today's replacement policy as its original authority.
- Typed reviewed findings, explicit unresolved prerequisites and opaque supporting evidence references/hashes/dates. A reference proves what the operator recorded; do not label it government verification. No copied raw passport, inbox messages or attachment bodies are needed.

Use a bounded revision chain, composite subject FKs, revision/request uniqueness, immutable ORM/bulk guards, byte/event limits and reserved withdrawal capacity. A new replacement is a new decision; never edit an earlier one. Reserve withdrawal using both count and bytes, and verify real boundary cases, not only reduced test constants.

### Fresh reconciliation and specialist ownership

Reuse `entry_interviews._header/_history` to validate the retained interview and `interview_rules.analyze/Answers` for current consent, freshness, conflicts and applicable fields. Add a **separate** versioned specialist field map/checker. Review current profile/interview/history contradictions instead of accepting an opaque “checked all” boolean. Decisions must not infer consent from a signed contract or profile.

Require an actual acknowledged handoff for a specialist's positive manual conclusion. ACK is acceptance of responsibility, not clearance. Known ordinary escalation (business/work/regularization) may be reviewed only within the delegated capability; illegal-service requests, privacy holds, unacknowledged ownership, unknown material facts and source uncertainty must retain their distinct consequences. A regularization specialist may record a quote for a lawful regularization service under explicit scope; an expired-stay stop cannot become permission to remain or restart the bot. Do not require `fresh.stops == []` for every manual outcome, nor allow one generic “override stops” checkbox.

`EntryInterview` is unique per case/applicant and its original `profile_id` is immutable. A later current profile must not force a second person/interview or rewriting that header. Validate the original header provenance, then explicitly reconcile the **current** interview answers against the latest profile/history in the new specialist decision. Corrections require real typed evidence; changing only an `as_of` date cannot erase a contradiction. Any later material head change invalidates the positive link until a fresh decision. Keep the stricter existing E628 behavior unchanged.

Use `applicant_stay_history.current_in_session` for the exact person; missing/withdrawn/incomplete history remains explicit. For a conclusion that asserts a stay limit or urgency, require the corresponding current source, factual attestation and approved calendar/counting checks. `current_stay_stops.evaluate_in_session` is reusable only within its exact attestation/head contract. Never reuse the dialogue-only reconciliation reader without its dialogue authority, or convert planned headroom into observed lawful stay. Merely recording an unknown deadline may be allowed for an incomplete estimate; claiming a computed deadline is not.

## Atomic quote linkage, locking and retained receipts

`entry_quotes.save_quote` currently opens its own transaction. Calling it after a separately committed decision read permits withdrawal/profile/source changes between checks. Extract a narrowly internal same-session save/preview reader from the existing implementation, preserving the public v1 API, command/result schema, calculator version, capacity and historical replay. Do not add `decision_id` to v1 command/pin JSON: `_history` validates an exact schema.

An additive decision→quote binding can be a quote action in the new journal or a small immutable binding table. Choose one, not both. It must retain the decision ID/hash, quote ID/hash, original request identities and same subject/application; the quote and binding commit together. A binding must not be backfilled automatically onto old arithmetic receipts. Existing standalone quotes retain their old meaning and cannot masquerade as decision-bound quotes.

For new writes, take the case lock, application locks in stable order when needed, applicant lock and existing publication set shared lock, then sample time. Existing profile/history/interview and application writers serialize on their parents; hold those locks through decision/quote/binding flush and commit. `knowledge_publication._runtime_context` and `_runtime_in_session` are the existing same-session source seam. They do not authorize the caller. Never call `runtime_knowledge` or another engine-owning wrapper inside this composition.

If consuming accepted package/quarantine evidence, respect the existing global observation-lock-before-case order used by `entry_processes.record_process_operation`; do not introduce case→observation inversion. Prefer not to consume acceptance evidence where an internal estimate only needs known pending prerequisites. Keep provider callbacks and ordinary inbox/logging outside this transaction.

Preview is read-only. Save rechecks exact current decision revision, profile/interview/history/application heads, capability/source/recipe, current delegation and day after lock acquisition. Quote-policy expiry at midnight, source review/publish/withdrawal, consent withdrawal and concurrent replacement must either serialize or reject the stale preview. No positive result from cached independent reads is sufficient.

Validate retained command identity and find exact duplicates before rejecting them because today's source/policy has changed; current identity/access is still mandatory. Original successful retries never reauthorize a new quote, replace the current decision or resend anything. Historical receipt validity and current usability are separate. Validate historical types, scope, chain, actor delegation, source identities, temporal order and semantic result—not just a recomputed attacker-controlled hash. Later decisions/source withdrawal must not erase original readable receipts. New replay versions must retain original interpretation or explicitly refuse unsupported replay; never silently recalculate old legal meaning under new code.

Withdrawal is a minimal stop operation with retained scoped authority, still possible after ordinary decision/quote policy expiry and at reserved capacity. It must not depend on current successful eligibility, available mutable inbox text or live external evidence. It changes the decision's current usability, not the arithmetic already recorded or the client's interview consent. Historical reads and exact retries survive full capacity; new ordinary writes fail explicitly.

### Exact Б.8 acceptance

**India, business 10 days:** latest individual IND/ordinary passport, real business-meeting purpose, actual host facts, exact ten-day plan, relevant foreign-residence and US/UK/Schengen long-term-visa facts, consistent current profile/interview and explicit source authority. Record which alternatives were examined; unresolved applicability stays unresolved. A seven-day exception does not approve ten days. Demonstrate delegated manual B planning→correct B recipe→same application's immutable internal quote. Source-example $50 state + $50 service and five working days are synthetic approved fixture data only unless separately approved; package/document collection and submission stay pending. A TS recipe, work-purpose reinterpretation, stale invitation/identity or another person's quote must fail.

**USA tourism 45 days:** reuse actual E628 30/60 whole-trip over-limit finding rather than a standalone calculation. Specialist alternatives require their own genuine purpose/history; no invented B/RL grounds or automatic travel split. NO_VISA or a tourist upsell must not create a fake visa application merely to access a quote.

**Uzbekistan, already in Bishkek 21 days, garment work:** exact personal actual entry/history and employer facts, specialist work route, factual urgency with approved calendar, and independently evidenced/time-bounded TPP concession. `employment.garment=True` does not prove membership or a discounted price. An EP quote is not permission to start work. Unknown stay end/calendar/benefit produces visible uncertainty, not a fabricated deadline or fee.

## Required negative and recovery controls

- Wrong case/person/application/attempt, payer/family substitution, stale passport continuity, mismatched head/hash or profile/interview answers; later correction after preview. Keep original IDs through E630A intake/contract without recertifying its behavior here.
- Wrong current actor, actor in interview/publisher/quote policy but absent from specialist delegation; wrong capability/recipe; missing/expired/replaced source policy; future review timestamp; publication withdrawal; source-page/catalog drift. Real defaults remain empty.
- Consent unknown/withdrawn, pending unacknowledged handoff, ACK with unresolved contradiction, applicable unmapped branch, purpose work→B/TS; a material privacy hold must not be overridden by specialist selection.
- Native open→review current facts→record decision→preview/save quote→reload exact receipt; no-JS and RU/EN meaning, mobile/contrast, read-only GET, CSRF/HMAC/actor/target binding, controlled 409/422/503 with original uncertain retry and no duplicate-backfill.
- Parallel actual PostgreSQL decision replacement/withdrawal, profile/history/interview updates, quote saves and publication/review updates; clock sampled after held lock. Two distinct commands at the same revision yield one winner. Same command returns one original result. A quote without its binding or vice versa must roll back, including cancellation and commit uncertainty.
- Rehashed malformed authority/types/revisions/source/quote references, future fact evidence, missing predecessor and inconsistent semantic result must fail closed. Correct original retries survive later source/policy removal and subsequent decisions without restoring usability. Capacity leaves room for withdrawal; historical rendering does not require a mutable raw inbox.
- Old standalone quote schema/result/history/retry and NO_VISA reader remain identical in meaning. Contract amounts, Decimal/unknown-price behavior, invoice/payment/refund/FX and channel suppression are untouched. Migration is additive; old journals survive migration and local backup/restore; populated rollback must not silently destroy retained evidence.

## Recommended bounded sequence and unresolved authority

First build the source index, explicit policy, subject-scoped journal/current reader and narrow native specialist review. Then compose decision-bound quote saving in one transaction and exercise the India example positively with synthetic authority plus the controls above. Finally extend **capability data and connected evidence** for the remaining SOP/manual outcomes, reusing the same infrastructure and existing numerical/document/process engines. Do not announce broad capability simply because all product codes fit the schema.

No new business decision is required to implement this empty-by-default local boundary. Actual authorized reviewers, clause approvals, price/urgent/refund/state-fee-payer rules, exception handling, calendaring interpretation and activation remain owner/source decisions. The policy stores explicit authority when supplied; code and synthetic fixtures cannot create it. An actual client-binding offer or automated broad eligibility would require another scoped design and approval, not a flag on this internal decision.
