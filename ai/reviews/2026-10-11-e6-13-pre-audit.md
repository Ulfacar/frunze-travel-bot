## Source implications

- Section 12.1 separates USD-denominated rates from KGS settlement on payment day. Do not copy illustrative som equivalents into authoritative fixed conversions. This slice can retain USD/KGS independently and explicitly leave settlement FX unavailable.
- Sections 12.1–12.2 distinguish fee base, additional 30-day blocks and urgency. An additional-block count is an explicit arithmetic parameter, not a decision about visa eligibility or calendar months. Source urgency differs by channel/location; an arbitrary global `urgent = ×3` is not adequate.
- The suggested bank markup is explicitly unverified. Bank fees must remain unknown until actually authorized, including currency if that is unknown. A known government/service subtotal is not a complete payable total.
- Sections 12.3–12.4 contain unverified/general tariffs, expiry-limited concessions and uncertainty about whether one fee includes another. Do not sum possibly included fees or infer an exemption. A source ambiguity must block completeness.
- Section 12.6 distinguishes company service fees and proposed decisions; section 12.7 explicitly omits bank charges in its examples. Synthetic TS/B examples should reproduce the known subtotal while still exposing unknown bank charges. Government urgency must not multiply service, bank or partner lines.
- Section 12.8 refund rules and section 12.9 payer/prepayment rules are outside quotation arithmetic. No quote write may create a payment/refund, agreed contract amount, payer assignment or paid/readiness fact.

## Architecture choice: recipes first, publication adapter separately

**Use a finite, versioned, trusted server recipe catalog in the default-empty QuotePolicy for this slice.** It declares required fee lines, source/business authority, applicability, exact formula and allowed parameters. Browser input selects an allowed recipe and supplies only bounded manual parameters. Store the selected immutable recipe and authority pins with each revision. Configuration proof strings are references to authority, not evidence that the agent has obtained real approval.

## Mandatory correctness conditions

### Monetary representation and recipe completeness

1. Use bounded canonical decimal strings with at most two fractional digits for this USD/KGS slice, explicitly allowing evidenced zero. Reject floats, booleans, negative or signed-zero forms if noncanonical, NaN/infinity, exponent tricks, excessive digits/precision and unsupported currencies. Bound operands before arithmetic and results after each operation; use an explicit Decimal context rather than ambient precision/rounding. `service_money._amount` at line 107 is a useful convention but rejects zero; do not change the payment validator to serve quotes.
2. Keep positive quantities distinct from the **nonnegative** count of additional blocks: zero extra blocks must work. One applicant per quote means person/application fee quantity is one; a generic quantity control must not reintroduce unscoped group quoting. Other quantities require recipe-declared meaning and bounds.
3. Prefer fixed amounts and `base + additional_count × increment`, with an explicitly authorized urgency multiplier at least one. If a decimal multiplier creates fractional cents, either reject that unsupported case or introduce a separately evidenced rounding rule; do not invent one. Multiply the complete eligible government line, including its increment, exactly once.
4. Every recipe must account for service/government/bank/partner, and allow multiple distinct lines within a component. Require stable unique IDs and full coverage. Missing component is invalid configuration; known zero, evidenced not-applicable and explicit unknown are different states. Required unknown lines cannot be suppressed through unchecked UI controls, omitted fields or recipe switching that contradicts the target procedure.
5. Each zero/NA/exemption needs specific evidence and a scope/condition. Unknown lines need a reason and no known amount. If their currency is unknown, represent that explicitly; do not invent USD just to fit a total bucket. Totals group known amounts by currency and component. When any required amount/currency/applicability is unresolved, the response must expose **known subtotals**, mark the quote incomplete and omit a complete payable total everywhere, including print/history/retry views.
6. Pin recipe procedure/product variant and permitted parameter combinations. At minimum reject a visa recipe on a unified-permit/registration application. TS/B category, multiplicity and duration are declared quote inputs or pinned recipe attributes; do not imply they were legally qualified from the generic application procedure. No automatic date-to-30-day-block conversion, fines/discounts/refunds or FX in this slice.

### Scope, source revisions and current state

7. Scope to exact case/application/applicant using existing validated identity and composite foreign keys. `entry_applications._application` at line 103 checks applicant/attempt identity, while `_events` at line 148 validates its journal. Derive the applicant from the selected application and cross-check any requested ID; never infer it from ServiceCase contact/payer. A successor attempt is a different quote target.
8. Pin the observed application event ID, revision and hash, plus application/applicant identity hashes. Reconstruct historical facts from that exact immutable prefix; do not demand that the old head is still the latest. Compare against the current head separately to mark old quotes stale. Define terminal/superseded application behavior explicitly; the smallest useful contract denies a new usable quote on a closed/refused or superseded attempt while preserving its historical calculations.
9. If no applicant profile/eligibility facts are consumed, state that manual quote parameters are unverified arithmetic inputs. If any such fact is used to select a tariff/exemption, pin its exact scoped version and invalidate current applicability when it changes. An application revision alone does not track profile/passport changes.
10. Distinguish historical arithmetic integrity, arithmetic completeness and current authority. A complete historical calculation can be expired/stale/withdrawn today. On missing, replaced, revoked or expired current policy/catalog, retain the old result but set current usability false. Even exact retry must not visually resurrect current authority: return the original immutable receipt with a separately recomputed current-status overlay.

### Transaction, replay and retry

11. Preview is read-only and never authoritative. Sign recipe/policy hash, complete parameters, exact target, application head, quote revision and preview/result hash. Save reauthorizes, locks, recomputes and compares that preview in one transaction. Do not silently save a new amount under an old preview. Check policy validity against the actual persisted timestamp after waiting for locks.
12. Preserve existing case → application lock order (`entry_applications.py:278`), and use one consistent transaction for quote history/CAS/source pins. Competing identical requests produce one receipt; distinct requests at the same expected revision have one winner. No source/table mutation outside the new journal is needed. `balance_from_rows` (`service_money.py:514`) is payment reconciliation, not a quote calculator; do not call ledger writes or `correct_contract_amount`.
13. Current authenticated identity/resource authorization precedes data access and retry lookup. For an authorized caller, an existing exact command/key is resolved before current quote policy/CAS/preview freshness checks. Policy or recipe disappearance must not make a committed request unrecoverable. Preserve the original command/policy pins for comparison; do not reconstruct its request hash from today's policy. A conflicting payload/key fails without returning another actor's receipt.
14. Replay every revision using its retained calculator version and exact normalized recipe/inputs; compare every line/state/subtotal/completeness flag and validate predecessor/scope/source pins. A matching hash chain alone is insufficient. Retain deployed old reducers in packaging and immutable recipe snapshots in storage/recovery artifacts. Test fully rehashed semantic tampering, while acknowledging that hashing is not external authenticity against an attacker able to replace all trusted policy/catalog/evidence anchors.
15. Bound line count, request/result/history bytes and revisions. The limit check for a new write follows exact retry resolution; a full journal must remain readable and retryable. Current views/history should not load all unrelated cases or depend on an ever-growing payment ledger. Validate DB JSON types, composite FK enforcement, unique revision/request constraints and ORM/bulk immutability on actual PostgreSQL.
