## Source and existing contract

The supplied source algorithm at `docs/kb-visa-inbound-v1.1-derived.md:810` requires passport country, document type, purpose, applicable regime, dates and rolling-window allowance, violation history, registration and then the final conclusion. In particular, step 5 at line 832 permits “visa not needed” only when the intended stay fits the remaining allowance. Product scope in source section 1.1 contains manager-only, partner and unsupported routes; a visa label alone does not establish an offered product.

The current `QualificationReview` deliberately emits no eligibility decision (`app/knowledge/qualification.py:203`). Its passport check tests expiry against request day, not expiry through the trip. Applicant profiles intentionally exclude payer stay history (`app/domain/applicant_profiles.py:44`). These restrictions must not silently disappear when connecting publication.

The condition schema is expressly draft-only (`app/knowledge/condition_schema.py:43`). `ConditionReview` checks predicate structure and bundle identity, not approved execution semantics. `PublicationPolicy` (`app/domain/knowledge_publication_rules.py:11`) approves source publication through named publishers; it does not separately authorize executable predicates or product routing. An active source publication alone is therefore insufficient to execute a legal interpretation.

## Required controls

1. **Separate executable approval.** A trusted server-side qualification policy must identify approval/evidence, algorithm version, validity/review dates, exact source, bundle, projection and condition-catalog identities, and explicit supported route mappings. Do not construct it from client input. Real configuration stays empty. A synthetic test approval must be unmistakably synthetic. A hash records identity, not legal correctness; actual approval remains a human gate.

2. **Complete competing-rule treatment.** Candidate discovery comes from the integrity-checked active bundle, not caller-selected unit IDs. Evaluate all relevant explicit, conditional, independent, unmapped and fallback regime alternatives. Unknown, unsupported, unresolved or plausible but unpublished alternatives prevent an unambiguous choice. Source `all_others` cannot become an executable catch-all by assumption. Any proof of irrelevance must follow approved executable semantics, not mere omission from publication.

3. **No loss of qualifiers.** `conditions.py` currently evaluates factual predicates but returns draft outcome, unresolved fields and `may_quote=False`. Reusing the result must retain unresolved source semantics. Approving a catalog with unsupported predicates, missing matching entries, missing country applicability or mismatched outcome must fail closed. Document-set completeness matters: absence of recorded visas/residence permits is not proof of absence. Entry-passport facts must override no unrelated or caller-supplied identity.

4. **Purpose and product restrictions.** Route mappings must include the actual purpose and relevant location/visa constraints. Tourism is not employment; a requested visa type does not prove suitability. Unsupported family, study, work, special documents, inside-country and manager-only cases require explicit fallback unless the exact required evidence and route semantics are supported. No implicit charge, product change, application creation, notification or customer promise follows a recommendation.

5. **One coherent database view.** Read and lock the exact applicant and current `KnowledgeSet` in one existing transactional operation. `_subject` already supplies an applicant shared lock and profile writers take its exclusive lock. Knowledge publication/decision writers take exclusive set locks. Separate calls to `read_applicant_profile` and `runtime_knowledge` release locks between calls and do not provide a coherent PG READ COMMITTED view. Lock ordering must agree with adjacent flows. Record the profile ID/revision/hash, active version, manifest, publication revision and policy digest in the response.

6. **Latest individual evidence and scope.** The public domain entry point authorizes the current actor and validates visa case/applicant scope before exposing data. A client-pinned obsolete profile is rejected or reported explicitly as stale. No payer/contact or sibling profile substitution. Preserve old stored snapshots and runtime hashes; put the new decision in a new adapter/contract rather than altering historical `QualificationReview` replay.

7. **Time and expiry.** Use one server clock instant after acquisition of relevant locks and the existing KG local-day convention. Do not use historical profile `as_of` as today's authority. Detect future profile/event clocks, stale travel dates, passport expiry before the intended trip completes, expired/current-basis facts, policy expiry and source/review expiry. A currently effective rule is not necessarily effective for the intended future trip; either require relevant effective-date coverage or explicitly withhold that future conclusion. Policy/source withdrawal or review change must invalidate subsequent reads.

8. **Stop reasons dominate.** Any complaint, refund/legal escalation, conflict, violation, special document/process or other established stop suppresses both selected regime and selected product. Questions must not restart automation during specialist handoff. At most three actual missing-fact questions, ordered by source dependency, in the selected language. Never turn `unknown`, stale, absent or not-applicable values into negative facts.

9. **Bounded claims about visa need.** Without complete individual stay-history/calculation and registration evidence, a possible regime or product route must retain explicit unresolved allowance/registration checks, `visa_required=None` where allowance controls that conclusion, no exact remaining-day or deadline assertion, and no entry guarantee. If the implementation deliberately stops at a potential route, document this acceptance limit rather than awarding the full source step-8 behavior. A separate visa-required regime may justify a bounded positive visa-requirement conclusion only if all competing conditional alternatives are resolved.

10. **Interview mapping is explicit.** Mapping typed interview facts must preserve source field/state/evidence identity, consent/paused state where applicable, and individual scope. Do not infer eligibility, inviter authority, document completeness or negative stop answers from unrecognized free text or missing fields. Changing prerequisite facts invalidates derived values. The existing interview handoff must remain intact.

11. **Native interface preserves the boundary.** Existing full-admin and feature gates apply on server routes. No-JS, both languages, mobile and print must show fallback, unresolved items, source/version and the provisional limits with equal prominence to the recommendation. Display uses escaped values and does not expose full personal snapshots unnecessarily. Browser/API query parameters cannot install approval policies or choose a more favorable source/profile.
