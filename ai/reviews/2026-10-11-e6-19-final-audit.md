At MAX_ACTIONS the same limit blocks escalation, completion and cancellation. This is reachable with ordinary valid operations: **250** distinct escalation receipts at the unmodified default limit leave the task active and the next cancellation raises `deadline_task_action_limit`. The installed native UI continues to offer those terminal actions, so this is a reachable lifecycle dead end rather than a hidden invalid-input path.

Smallest compatible correction discussed: ordinary MAX_ACTIONS remains unchanged, but allow one extra terminal receipt, read at most MAX_ACTIONS+2 to detect overflow, preserve old exact retries, and rely on validated terminal status to refuse any second fresh terminal action. This also preserves existing legitimately full histories without requiring rewriting or backfill.
