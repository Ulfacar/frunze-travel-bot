## Concrete implementation gaps and boundaries

### 1. Work must consume own actual stay without requiring a fictitious visa

`entry_process_rules.fact_role:113` maps every work `actual_entry_confirmed` fact to role `visa`; `entry_process_evidence.issued_fact:283` accepts only visa/exit-visa attempts. The current work test creates and approves a separate visa, then writes an entry travel fact before W15. Source §19.9 explicitly includes a visa-free worker already in KG, including the UZ golden. Copying that fixture would falsify the example.

Smallest correction: a versioned, explicit work evidence adapter reads the person's current personal stay history in the **same session** and locks, pins the exact profile/passport/history head and evidenced actual entry, and distinguishes it from planned travel. Do not import payer/sponsor history or use the profile's `location='in_kg'` alone. Missing/unknown/withdrawn history, wrong person/passport, future entry, closed stay and changed head must fail closed. Where factual verification beyond self-report is required, reuse explicit fact authority rather than pretending a history row is legal permission. Preserve the visa-bearing path and historical v1 read/retry semantics.

### 2. EP-period registration cannot satisfy RES-period registration

`fact_role` maps registration facts at W15 and W20 to one `registration` role. Used roles cannot be rebound (`entry_process_rules:129`). The existing dependency test approves its registration before W15 and later reaches W20 with that same approval. This proves phase continuity, not the source's fresh registration on the RES basis.

Smallest correction: distinct immutable phase links/evidence (for example EP-registration and RES-registration) or a separately versioned registration epoch with equivalent strict identity. Require the post-RES fact to name the current resident basis/decision and its own valid period; chronology alone is insufficient. Preserve old link meaning. Negatives: old certificate, pre-card approval, new unrelated registration, other applicant, wrong passport/basis, changed or withdrawn RES evidence. Missing second registration must not prevent reading old history, but must prevent fresh readiness.

### 3. Dismissal is a source flag, not currently an executable branch

The process catalog contains WX1 with the source notification/annulment/exit text but **no edge to or from WX1**. `edge_for` rejects target stages of `kind='flag'`. A test asserting that the string is in the catalog would not exercise §19.9 step 14.

Use a bounded real operational dismissal event/branch with its own dated evidence and tasks, without changing the meaning of all source flags or inferring an official annulment from employer notice. Preserve record/history after termination; absence of an official decision stays explicit. Test 3-calendar-day/30-day timing, canceled/changed facts, wrong employer/person and no automatic legal clearance. Other source flags need only their relevant connected coverage; do not expand this into a general unbounded workflow system.

### 4. Exact EP submission/renewal due dates need their own authority

`current_stay_checks.working_margin` explicitly proves **<5 versus ≥5** and stops at five; `exact=False` is a lower bound. It cannot compute the UZ golden's ten-working-day EP submission deadline. `entry_deadline_sources` currently anchors application events or issued travel. Its manual operational date requires actual portal/official-document evidence; its calculated source review is a draft candidate (`publication_approved=False`). All 22 original `kind='deadline'` units inspected still have `value=null` and unverified source text in `blocked_value`.

Smallest coherent addition: a closed, separately authorized derived-deadline operation/reader that pins the exact source clause/rule, actual basis/end evidence, case/person/profile/history identity, approved calendar/year hashes and endpoint interpretation. Distinguish a **derived reminder/submission deadline** from an officially supplied date and legal permission. Reuse pure calendar arithmetic and existing durable task reconciliation where their input contract fits. Do not invent an official-document reference for a calculated date, fill the 22 real draft values, broaden current-stop helper semantics, or take a planned exit/visa validity as actual lawful-until.

For the first work slice, implement only the exact EP-before-visa-free-end rule needed by Б.8 plus clearly enumerated lifecycle rules required by its accepted SOP slice. Other source timings can follow in a separate closed mapping, including calendar versus working days/months. Tests must include weekend, approved holiday, year crossing, missing calendar, anchor changed/withdrawn, late submission, boundary equal day, ambiguous endpoint and exact retry after time/source changes. Unknown authority produces a specialist task with no invented due date.

### 5. Keep manual procedure evidence and current source decisions distinct

Existing process manual attestations pin the profile and their own authorized source/reference; they do not automatically validate E628/E630B receipts. A scenario may honestly use an authorized manual review. If a new link claims to consume a current qualification/specialist result, it needs a real application adapter with exact case/person/profile/interview/history/source/policy pins and same-session currentness checks. Copying a boolean or embedding a hash in free text is not that adapter. Add only the specific consumer required; a second all-product specialist-decision journal is not justified by J01 alone.
