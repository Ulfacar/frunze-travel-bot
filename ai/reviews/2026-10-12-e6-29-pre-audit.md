## Minimum complete H02 change

1. A bounded RU/EN incoming-interruption adapter, attached before normal question/FAQ/advice generation for the explicitly authorized KG scope. It must recognize the source families above and human-requested stop/consent withdrawal even when the message does not answer the displayed question. Uninterpretable or contradictory safety-relevant input produces explicit uncertainty and specialist handling; a successful slot parser or keyword match is not proof of a complete free-text classifier. Use local synthetic messages and deterministic/fake dependencies for verification; no new paid provider is necessary.
2. Trusted, explicit route-to-case/applicant/interview context while the interview is still active. Neither payer `Dialog.contact_id` nor the permanent `EntryChannelStop` can supply that identity by inference. Ambiguous/shared-contact cases cannot silently select the first applicant. The new registration/adapter authority may remain default-empty and synthetic locally.
3. Fresh stop evaluation: current interview/profile/assessment and applicable approved calendar/history, with provenance and bounded specialist reasons. A pinned old profile at interview start is not the latest qualification. Calendar/STOP12 unknowns must stop advice rather than produce an exact invented threshold result.
4. One durable connected transition from accepted trigger to pending handoff and exact-route suppression, preserving frozen card and later consent-withdrawal evidence. The source reason/current revisions must be inspectable. Reuse existing suppression/ACK, do not create a second competing send guard. Existing pending/acked cards and permanent stops must remain stopped after correction, ACK, stale retries or policy expiry.

This is the minimum complete scope, not a recommendation to implement all57 SOP actions or global/multichannel handling. An operator-only new button would improve the local flow but leave arbitrary client interrupts incomplete.

## Critical reuse and recovery pitfalls

- **P1 identity/authority:** case contact proves payer association, not applicant identity. A transport latch blocks the entire exact route; if one phone represents multiple people, do not claim per-person selective sending on that route. Never accept actor/owner/route/source approvals from message text or form hidden fields alone.
- **P1 transaction/order:** `bind_stop` takes route advisory lock, registry-capacity lock, then case/Dialog; `record_action` takes case. Calling public writers sequentially leaves a send window; nesting them can invert locks and own transactions. Compose within one transaction in existing order; no provider calls inside retryable writers. Sample policy day/time after waits.
- **P1 retained semantics:** `_registry:161` requires generic `action=='handoff'` when the observed phase was active/paused. Merely replacing it with `interrupt` invalidates old validation assumptions. Keep v1 reducers/cards/receipts byte- and behavior-compatible; separately version new cause/connection evidence or add an explicit retained validator. Do not rewrite `channel_suppression` in historic snapshots.
- **P1 uncertain delivery:** send-first may finish one already admitted callback before the writer acquires its lock; bind-first must block the callback. State this precise linearization contract, not impossible retraction. Timeout/cancellation/cleanup failure must never reinvoke the provider. Failure before commit must not claim a saved stop, manager ACK or delivered notification.
- **P1 consent and pending state:** stop collection after refusal/withdrawal even after ACK. Preserve the original card; a later correction cannot erase the reason or reopen automation. A new raw-message adapter must not route consent withdrawal into legacy silent fact extraction.
- **P2 capacity/recovery:** interview reserves terminal events, channel registry is bounded at128 stops/4MiB. New cause journal/registration needs explicit bounds and terminal reserve. At full capacity, do not report a stop that was rolled back or let the triggering turn fall through into advice; define the operational failure state and durable recovery. Existing unrelated unbound routes are currently admitted, so no global safety claim follows from a failed new binding.
- **P2 activation versus policy:** expired/removed binding policy does not resume saved latches; removing deployment activation currently bypasses this guard for that account. These are different controls. Preserve the present contract unless an explicit owner decision authorizes a change. Missing/corrupt DB on an activated route must fail closed, including a negative lookup.
