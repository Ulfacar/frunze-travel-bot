## Critical reuse and recovery pitfalls

- **P1 identity/authority:** case contact proves payer association, not applicant identity. A transport latch blocks the entire exact route; if one phone represents multiple people, do not claim per-person selective sending on that route. ### Limit factual authority rather than making it a second qualification engine

The proposed fact authority is acceptable as a **bounded, server-owned attestation for one current person/version**, not a generic dictionary that overrides profile, interview, history or calculated rule outcomes. A single attestation may cover a canonical set of clearly named facts; separate signatures for every leaf are not necessary if its authority explicitly covers that whole set, including completeness/absence assertions.

Require exact case/applicant identity and immutable applicant pin; latest profile ID/hash and current source version; opaque passport reference; observation date/timestamp and validity; actor/authority/reference/proof; the facts' canonical hash. Check current operator authority and subject scope as appropriate to the public read wrapper. A hash/reference records provenance, not an external signature or proof of government verification. All policy objects must come from trusted server configuration/composition, never raw HTTP/LLM input. Keep byte/list bounds and reject duplicates, extra fields and ambiguous attestations rather than picking the first or latest by convenience.

The attestation may assert supported observed inputs: current location, actual current basis, a precisely defined permission-end fact, and supported condition-document inventories. It must **not** assert `selected_regime`, `eligible`, `remaining_days`, `safe_to_continue`, `no_stop`, `overstay=False`, or “all other regimes excluded”. Derive those bounded check outcomes from the approved source and validated observations. Passport country/document type must agree with the pinned profile; current-entry/basis dates and passport reference must agree with relevant history and current supported interview facts. Any overlapping contradiction produces a reasoned conflict, not precedence of one source over another. A later profile revision, history withdrawal/correction, passport change or materially conflicting interview fact invalidates the fresh result even if the old attestation is still within its nominal validity period.

**The meaning of `lawful_until` needs a strict definition.** A visa's validity/end-of-entry period, allowed duration of stay, planned departure, passport expiry and registration expiry are different facts. Copying any field called `valid_until` is not enough. Accept only an evidence-bound actual permission-end fact whose meaning is covered by this authority, or leave the expiry branch unknown. If an operator attests that date after reviewing the basis, label it as an operator attestation, not a computed or government-verified deadline. Registration must never manufacture or extend permission to stay. Unsupported/none/unknown basis remains explicit.

`FACTS_SCHEMA` itself includes potentially powerful assertions: `foreign_visas.complete`, `residence_documents.complete`, `over_three_years_verified`, `tourism_authority_petition`, and `stay_within_one_month_verified`. Whitelist the subset this helper actually supports. Do not make missing collections `complete=True/items=[]`; a complete-empty inventory requires the explicit attestation, and incomplete/missing stays unknown. Do not accept the “within one month verified” shortcut as an executable calculation; if a manual competing regime depends on it, retain a manual/unknown result unless its actual bounded interpretation and evidence are supported. A case-level payer fact cannot satisfy any of these individual predicates.
