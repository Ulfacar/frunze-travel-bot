## Source-first assessment

- **Person/profile identity:** the journal never loads payer stay history. Case/applicant/profile/predecessor composite foreign keys, actor-bound commands, previous ID/hash and profile ID/hash provenance are checked. New saves require the latest validated individual profile. Historical pins and exact retries resolve their original profile. A later profile yields `profile_changed`; it does not rewrite or silently refresh previous evidence.
- **Passport and uncertainty:** the current passport is an explicit nullable 32-hex opaque reference. Country and application-issued 64-hex fingerprints are not used to infer identity. Changing the current reference preserves old interval references and adds a continuity-review finding. Missing history differs from explicitly complete empty history. Missing completeness, country, reference, basis or confirmation remains visible. A confirmed client statement is rejected. Closed overlaps are retained under the existing schema; no new legal counting rule is invented. Even `recorded_inputs` explicitly has `eligibility_evaluated=False` and `may_quote=False`.
- **Chronology and retries:** normalization rejects contradictory interval dates and multiple open intervals. New-write server time is sampled after parent locks/history validation. A future local `as_of`, time before the pinned profile, or reversed journal clock blocks a new write. Older as-of evidence needs current confirmation. Existing actor/body/key matching receipt is resolved before new profile/date/CAS/capacity checks, preserving delayed retries.
- **Coherence and integrity:** writers take case then applicant locks; readers hold a shared applicant lock while validating complete history and pinned profiles. The internal `current_in_session` reader uses the caller's existing transaction/verified subject and does not open another transaction or use a payer calculator. Callers must obey its documented parent-lock contract. Every bounded row is validated before current or historical results; malformed history cannot be bypassed by asking for an older revision. ORM and bulk-write immutable guards register the new model.
- **Limits and correction:** maximum 250 revisions, 1 MiB canonical event, 16 MiB journal. Ordinary saves reserve one revision and 8,192 bytes for withdrawal. Preflight bounds SQL JSON text before decoding, followed by exact canonical byte limits. Withdrawal stores a small marker retaining the preceding profile pin. Old versions and exact receipts remain readable. A withdrawal is not deletion or a migration repair.
- **Native operations:** authenticated full-admin, feature and workday gates precede domain access. Signed actor/session/case/person/revision/profile/base hash and stable retry key protect form scope. Strict fields, duplicate-field rejection, 8 KiB body and bounded value limits exclude raw passport-number/upload fields. Server-side interval operations preserve undisplayed entries rather than round-tripping a truncated whole-history form. Editing visits clears completeness. Historical views are read-only and distinguish historical facts from current findings. Echo is escaped; unavailable/corrupt reads suppress editable forms.
- **Migration:** 0035 adds only the new journal and composite constraints. Existing contact/profile rows are neither converted nor copied. SQLite and PostgreSQL populated downgrade refuses loss under an appropriate transaction/table lock; offline downgrade refuses because it cannot verify data. No production migration is in scope.
