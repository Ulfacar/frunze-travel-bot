## Required authority boundary

`QualificationPolicy/1` approves executable predicate-to-product routing only.
It does not approve day-count conventions, registration interpretation,
calendars or interview reconciliation. `StayDayReview` deliberately returns a
proposed scenario with `counting_policy_not_approved` and
`rule_selection_not_evaluated`. Keep that schema, algorithm and historical
replay intact. Do not edit its result to say approved.

Use a separate immutable server-owned assessment authority, empty by default,
with its own format/algorithm, reference/proof, digest, valid-from/review-due,
exact `QualificationPolicy.digest`, source/bundle/projection/catalog hashes,
and explicit supported scope. It must contain or pin these independently
reviewable mappings:

1. Connected regime applicability/disposition for the complete source VF/VR/SP
   set, described below; source unit IDs/hashes and approved interpretation.
2. Counting convention: entry/exit inclusion, backward-inclusive window,
   Asia/Bishkek date basis, selected arithmetic algorithm and basis/passport
   scope. Reference `KG.RULE.COUNT_ENTRY_DAY`, `KG.RULE.COUNT_EXIT_DAY` and
   `KG.RULE.WINDOW_ANCHOR`; those actual draft values are null and
   `decision_pending`. A separately approved convention must be displayed as
   that authority's interpretation, never as an already confirmed PDF value.
   If an executable source value exists, require exact agreement. An unresolved
   contradiction cannot be bypassed by a configuration boolean.
3. Registration semantics and exact country coverage, with source references
   and units kept separate by kind. The first release can support an explicit
   exemption-only positive branch and source-grounded referral branches.
4. Stop/margin handling, including `KG.ESC.STOP_12` from PDF 4.1: remaining
   allowance at or below 3 days requires a manager. Bind the approved threshold
   and how it applies across the planned daily window results. Mathematical
   non-exceedance alone is insufficient.
5. Interview bridge version, exact catalog and current interview policy hash,
   supported field mapping, allowed completion state and nondecision fields.

Existing publication policy remains independently required. An authority
reference/proof is provenance, not verification of an external signature; tests
must label their authority synthetic. Real configuration stays empty until the
appropriate owner supplies approval. A form must not supply any of these
authorities, rule IDs, counts, publication state or an `approved` flag.

## Full source scope: a necessary extension beyond E6-22

The old route service globally rejects any special regime, general
`all_others`, unresolved outcome or unsupported month predicate. The actual
bundle contains these. Connecting the old service unchanged to arithmetic can
therefore never produce a supported result for the full bundle, even where a
particular applicant is plainly outside those other scopes.

Do not silently weaken the old API. Give the connected assessment an explicit
versioned contract for **every** VF/VR/SP unit, classified as executable or
manual-only. Require exact complete ID/hash coverage, with no unclassified or
extra source unit. A manual-only unit needs a separately approved, source-bound
applicability predicate or exclusion proof; an unconditional `ignore` flag is
not sufficient.

- A manual predicate that is true or unknown forces specialist review. A false
  predicate can exclude that unit only with current known mapped facts and
  whole-trip-valid applicability authority.
- Exclusion approval is narrower than approval of the regime's unavailable
  outcome. A blocked/null outcome may remain manual-only; neither its contents
  nor `blocked_value` are executable merely because an exclusion was approved.
  Keep evidence of the exact source bytes and the distinct scope approval.
- `KG.SP.CHN_IND_LONGTERM_VISA`, `KG.SP.CHN_TOUR_GROUP`, `KG.SP.HK_MACAO`,
  `KG.SP.UN_LP`, and `KG.VR.SIMPLIFIED_GCC_RESIDENT_TS` cannot be dropped.
  Country, document, residence and group facts must prove their exclusion under
  the approved mapping; unknown residence/group facts cannot count as false.
- `KG.VR.GENERAL` has `countries: all_others`. Its exclusion requires an explicit
  approved country partition/default interpretation. Do not infer a complement
  from whichever list was successfully loaded or decide that the first
  matching VF automatically wins. Multiple applicable executable regimes still
  require fallback unless a separately approved disambiguation exists.
- All potentially applicable executable competitors must have current
  published/reviewed values covering the entire trip. Withdrawal, changed
  interpretation/source hashes or expired authority invalidates the result.

A positive test must preserve the hard regimes and source structure instead of
deleting them from a minimal fixture. Synthetic review/publication/authority is
permitted for this evidence; silently sanitizing the source universe is not.
If this extension is deferred, E6-28 can be useful but C05 stays UNKNOWN.

## Registration contract and concrete source distinctions

| Source | Meaning for this assessment | Unsupported inference |
|---|---|---|
| `KG.REG.EXEMPT.<ISO3>`, kind `registration_exemption`, `value.days` | Explicit exemption span under approved boundary counting | Registration term, visa-free allowance, or an actual deadline |
| `KG.REG.TERM.<ISO3>`, kind `registration_term`, `value.days` | Period for which registration can be issued | Days permitted without registration |
| `KG.REG.DEFAULT`, `deadline_working_days_from_border`, explicit `not_in_list` | Default submission rule only for explicitly covered countries | Complement of missing/failed country lookup, or extra days after exemption |
| `KG.DL.REGISTRATION_OUTSIDE_LIST` | Separate deadline source; draft value is null | A ready five-working-day calculation |
| `KG.DL.VISA_FREE_REGISTRATION` | Separate exemption deadline/variants; draft value is null | Generic exemption-days-plus-five formula |
| `KG.RULE.REG_DOES_NOT_EXTEND_VF` | Registration does not enlarge the VF allowance | Registration makes an exceeded trip permissible |

The actual draft has `KG.REG.EXEMPT.RUS.days=30`,
`KG.VF.EAEU.days=90/window_days=180`; they are different limits and neither is
currently approved. MDA exemption is blocked/null while `KG.REG.TERM.MDA=90`;
the latter cannot repair the former. UZB term 60 and DEFAULT five working days
are different concepts and must not be collapsed. Treat ambiguous exemption vs
default coverage as a conflict, not choose the shortest/first value.

Require exact relevant registration coverage against the immutable source
set. A term alone is not exemption/default coverage; absence is unknown unless
the approved mapping explicitly establishes non-membership. Preserve conflicting
units and identify them in the fallback.

For the first positive path, a planned trip wholly within a current approved
exemption can yield `within_planned_exemption`, with its counting convention
and source. It does not establish a registration fact or actual deadline.
For a trip over exemption or a DEFAULT country, return the supported
registration obligation/referral and preserve `actual_deadline=None` until
actual entry plus separately approved anchor/calendar semantics exist. Do not
invent a deadline from planned entry. Calendar completeness is not calendar
approval; missing years, provisional calendars, unsupported month/boundary
semantics or unapproved working-day rules require explicit uncertainty.

## Typed interview: minimal complete, reachable bridge

Validate the whole header/event journal using its retained catalog/reducer.
Require current configured interview authority matching its pinned authority,
authorized current operator, and source pin matching the **latest** profile.
Re-run `rules.analyze` at the assessment date and use `Answers.get`/fresh
dependency stamps, not raw values or the historical cached analysis.

The recommended first positive state is `acked` with a handoff whose sole
reason is `intake_complete`, fresh analysis with no gaps/stale/stops/conflicts,
complete data consent, `collection_blocked=False` and no consent withdrawal.
This is reachable: the old reducer automatically moves a complete active
interview into `pending`, then the owner ACKs it. Requiring both `active` and
complete would make the positive path unreachable.

ACK is not legal approval and does not prove present consistency. Owner
`correct/remove` remains possible after ACK; the handoff card is not replaced.
For the narrow contract require the current used answers still match the
acknowledged complete card, as well as the current profile/history. A changed
material answer, stale parent stamp or different profile pin returns fallback.
Do not pretend there is a reset/re-ACK mechanism when the old reducer supplies
none. Future recovery of such a completed interview is separate workflow work.

`absent`, `active/incomplete`, `paused`, pending handoff and stopped/withdrawn
must remain distinct. Conservative initial behavior for absence is “complete
the individual interview”; it is not evidence that there are no contrary
answers. Manager/unsafe/legal/calendar/uncertainty interrupts cannot be treated
as ordinary completion. Privacy consent and offer acceptance stay separate;
offer acceptance is not permission to quote, charge or submit.

needs copying into the assessment. Work/inviter, family/minor, in-KG, special
residence/visa and event branches are outside the first positive scope unless
their material fields receive an explicit mapping.

## Positive path, C05 boundary and acceptance

A reasonable first positive fixture is an ordinary adult RUS applicant abroad,
individual tourism for 10 days, exact current profile, current complete empty
personal history and current same-passport reference, complete matching ACKed
interview, unique approved `KG.VF.EAEU`, approved 90/180 counting and stop
mapping, and approved RUS 30-day registration exemption. The full source-shaped
VF/VR/SP universe must remain present and be safely resolved/excluded under the
new authority. This demonstrates internal **NO_VISA regime qualification for
that recorded plan**, not permission to enter or a quote. A transit branch must
also distinguish border entry from airside transit; current profiles do not
encode that distinction, so tourism is the safer initial positive scope.

To justify C05 beyond renaming `candidate`, require a second positive with
nonempty confirmed same-passport history, and paired inputs where changing
personal dates/history changes the overall selected result. Also cover at
least another window/limit regime and a registration obligation/conflict
branch. A fully supported result must explicitly select an internal regime/
product after profile, competitors, history, entire-trip arithmetic,
registration and interview checks. Keep `may_quote=False` and no client
admission guarantee. A preserved candidate may be shown separately on fallback
but must not populate the final selected result.

This implements the distinction in PDF 2.1 steps 5–8: a country-level VF route
does not settle the person's proposed trip. An over-limit plan must exit the
NO_VISA selected result into purpose/other-basis review; B.8 USA 45 days cannot
remain a qualified VF 45-day trip merely because the underlying country rule
is known. The result should separately expose regime, visa/product conclusion
within supported scope, route/source, duration findings, registration and
risks; unsupported application method or legal deadlines remain explicit.

The fixed criterion does not say “automate every visa type.” Its explicit
uncertainty clause permits justified specialist outcomes. It does not permit
an always-fallback implementation or an artificially reduced source set.
Record a requirement/evidence table for PDF blocks 1/2/4/8 before final scoring:

- Tourism within a supported allowance: actual internal selection with source
  and personal evidence. Tourism over allowance (B.8 USA 45 days), at margin,
  or registration unresolved: no permission/no invented B or TS, clear cause.
- India business 10 days: current E6-22 only supports ordinary tourism TS for
  VR. B.8 expects business B consideration and special-document checks. A
  specific “business route needs approved B mapping/manager” is honest
  uncertainty, but is **not** evidence that B is implemented. If the agreed
  acceptance requires that routine approved business route to select B, add a
  new source-bound B mapping with inviter/material facts and its own positive
  control; do not claim its selection via a label or tourism policy.
- Work/UZB, in-KG renewal/change/overstay, special documents, family and unsafe
  requests: show the applicable source/profile/interview stop and specialist
  branch. PDF 4.1 explicitly requires many of these referrals. Do not suggest
  TS from inside KG or treat a work request as tourism.

Thus a bounded but substantive connected **regime** implementation can satisfy
the fixed technical C05 contract if the above full-source and uncertainty
coverage is agreed and evidenced; it does not establish all-product automation,
B.8 business completion or J01/J02 golden end-to-end acceptance. If business
selection is included in C05's accepted product matrix, that branch is required
before awarding the whole 2 points. The reviewer must state the chosen matrix
explicitly, rather than hide that requirement after implementation.
