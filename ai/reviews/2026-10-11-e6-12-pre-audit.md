## Actual gaps and implications

1. **Existing intake is neither the complete questionnaire nor a durable interview.** `app/knowledge/intake.py:2` groups absent, unknown, not-applicable and empty string together. `qualification.py` derives at most three questions from a profile, but has no durable question/answer/handoff progression. The current slots schema validates a relatively complete profile; several conditional requirements are unsuitable for incremental answers. Do not reuse these semantics as the v3 state machine.

2. **A code-label inventory alone would overstate completeness.** The source has 57 question codes, many containing several independent fields. Current structures do not completely represent, for example, social-contribution debt (F4), workplace (F5), existing/planned company or IP ownership (F9), family document/principal/child/dependant details (G), payment method and company closing-document needs (H). Versioned v3 must map atomic subfields and conditions back to all A–H source codes. Preserve old schemas and replay rules.

3. **Contact evidence is not applicant evidence.** `app/domain/models.py:821` makes the ServiceCase contact the payer/contact; `app/domain/applicant_profiles.py:43` deliberately avoids treating payer history as the applicant's history. `qualification_storage.py:80` stores contact-scoped qualification. A shared payer, family or business contact must not merge applicants' passports, location, history, consent or qualification. The new aggregate must be scoped to the exact case and applicant, with scoped foreign keys and authorization on every command/read. Any attached applicant qualification must pin that exact applicant profile/evidence version. Contact qualification may be displayed as explicitly identified contact context, not applicant qualification.

4. **Existing manager notification is not durable acceptance.** `app/core/instant_handoff.py:137` claims notification using `Conversation.handoff_notified_at`; `maybe_notify` at line 217 is best-effort delivery machinery. It does not establish an immutable card, assigned accepting owner or acknowledgement CAS. `_maybe_wait_ack` in orchestrator sends a waiting message to the client; it is not manager acknowledgement. Reuse neither label as evidence of the new acknowledgement requirement.

5. **Stopping new drafts does not stop the installed bot.** `app/core/orchestrator.py:579` sends through `self.channel.send` at line 595. `app/core/followup.py:130` independently selects and sends followups through `outbound.send_to_client` at line 139. That outbound function (`app/channels/outbound.py:29`) is also used by actual human responses (`app/admin/router.py:2031,2059`) and operational alerts (`awaiting.py:273`, `watchdog.py:128`). A blanket check there could wrongly block human/operational actions, while an orchestrator-only check misses followups. Current intercept may expire (`app/core/intercept.py:43`); process-local locks do not establish a durable cross-process handoff boundary.

6. **Canonical Dialog is not already a proven provider route.** `Dialog` (`models.py:119`) has channel/bot/key identity and contact, but no case/applicant binding. `shadow_bridge.py:54–58` and `messaging_shadow.py:41–45` use normalized phone as its key and are best-effort shadow paths; this key must not be assumed equal to the provider's `Message.chat_id`. Sharing the case contact proves a communication relationship, not that the applicant personally owns that route. `OutboxJob` is a shadow ledger, not the actual delivery queue.

## Required core architecture

### Typed, versioned answers and bounded progression

- Pin a complete catalog version/hash, explicit rule version, language and source mapping. Include RU/EN prompts and the reason for sensitive questions. Start with the seven primary source topics, then applicable details; stop factors take precedence over ordinary next questions.
- Distinguish absent, answered unknown, concrete values (including false, zero and meaningful empty lists), and not-applicable. Not-applicable requires a proven condition; an unknown prerequisite leaves a gap. Reject structurally invalid values without writing. Preserve structurally valid but conflicting facts and atomically escalate when appropriate; rejecting the whole answer must not discard a stop factor.
- Answered unknown may remove a question from immediate repetition, but must not satisfy readiness or be silently converted to false/not-applicable. Exhausting currently askable questions with gaps produces an incomplete review/handoff, not a fabricated qualified result.
- A batch contains at most three canonical answer paths and must match the exact signed displayed question set, case/applicant, revision and policy/catalog. Server code recomputes the accepted paths and outcome. Corrections are explicit commands with the same typed bounds; do not accept a browser-provided readiness result. Bound nested arrays/objects and aggregate bytes, not just the outer field count. Stable member identifiers prevent an array reorder from retargeting a correction.
- Changing a prerequisite does not erase its prior dependent facts. Retain them as stale/inapplicable history until explicit correction/removal; exclude stale facts from current readiness. Pause/resume and language changes do not clear stop state or consent history.
- Do not derive legal stay thresholds, calendar deadlines, guaranteed outcomes, prices or confidence scores without separately approved actual sources. Section 17 is an escalation/reference source, not authorization to implement autonomous legal SOPs here. H03/C04/C05 remain separate.

### Consent and PII boundary

The PDF's prohibition on requesting scans before product/consent is not a complete privacy/retention policy for collecting basic personal data. Existing Contact data, full-admin access and a synthetic policy fixture are not proof of the applicant's consent or of lawful live processing.

- Policy must be absent/empty by default; pin its identity, source/privacy authority references, catalog hash, permitted existing operators and validity. No real approval should be seeded. Local fixtures remain synthetic.
- Define an explicit policy allowlist for any collection before consent; default to no authorized personal fields when that policy is absent. The report does not invent a business/legal pre-consent exception. Contact reuse also needs a permitted purpose and clear provenance.
- Separate processing consent from offer/terms acceptance even if source code A6 groups them. Preserve explicit affirmative/refused/unknown status, policy/version and evidence/time. Unknown/refusal must never become consent merely to advance the interview. Consent for a contact/payer is not automatically consent for each family member or applicant.
- Only collect authorized, necessary fields for the current phase. No scans, criminal-case narratives or medical diagnoses. Record only the permitted escalation indicator; dependant questions must not become medical-history fields. Use field-specific limits/rationale rather than a universal unrestricted notes box.
- Withdrawal blocks further collection/drafts; it must remain possible to record a minimal handoff/operational stop without forcing more personal answers. Immutable history does not by itself implement erasure or retention obligations. Real retention, deletion and consent authority remain human-policy gates before live use.

### Durable handoff, owner and immutable history

- Persist answer plus resulting pending handoff atomically before returning any next draft. Include explicit interrupts and source stop factors, including complaint/refund, legal/unsafe requests and manual manager request. Allow interrupts while paused or with incomplete facts. No additional narrative is required to trigger escalation.
- Freeze exact facts, gaps, reasons, catalog/policy versions, source evidence pins and interview revision in the card. Unsupported qualification, fee or confidence stays unknown. Later corrections must not rewrite the card or make it appear to contain the latest facts.
- Pending and acknowledged states produce no autonomous question/advice drafts. Acknowledgement, restart, policy expiry/replacement or ordinary language/resume commands cannot silently re-enable them. Later specialist fields are manual owner actions; label their provenance and show their relation to the frozen card separately.
- Owner must be a designated existing full-admin identity. Validate it against trusted current access configuration, not an arbitrary supplied login. Acknowledge the exact pending handoff/card hash with CAS; no different owner, manager-only role or cross-case actor may accept it. Define explicitly whether specialist writes require acknowledgement; the smallest safe contract is to require it. This must not reassign `ServiceCase.owner_login`, create permissions, call Bitrix or notify a real manager.
- Reserve event-count and byte capacity for handoff plus acknowledgement, including at the maximum normal history size. Repeated no-op terminal commands must not consume reserved capacity. Normal collection and policy expiration cannot make terminal operational actions unavailable. Reads/retries must not require loading every case/contact/journal in the database.
- Each event uses a deterministic versioned reducer. Replay validates scope, contiguous revision, operation semantics, question set, typed answer, policy/catalog pins and resulting state, not just a hash chain. Select exact immutable source versions; later source growth must not reinterpret an old event. Current access/policy availability is an overlay, not a reason to corrupt historical replay.
- Authenticate and verify requested resource scope before idempotency lookup. An authorized exact retry must be resolved before current-policy expiry, CAS or fresh-question rejection; it returns the original event/result/card, not a new latest draft. Conflicting reuse of a request key fails. Separate current eligibility to make a new change from permission to retrieve a previously committed result.

## Explicit H04 boundary and later integration gate

For this slice, the useful deliverable is a persistent local interview and acknowledged manual-review queue that suppresses its own returned drafts. **It must not advertise that the existing reply/followup channels have stopped. H04 earns no points here.** B03/B04/H01/H02 and related intake criteria also earn only the coverage actually demonstrated after implementation; a complete catalog alone is insufficient.

If channel integration is later proposed, its minimum design needs a separately approved exact route binding and all of these checks:

1. Prove existing Dialog/contact/case mapping and provider route canonicalization; pin identity and reject changes. Do not guess by phone/name or equate payer with applicant. Explicitly state the route coverage; any pending bound interview on a shared route must prevent another interview from bypassing the stop.
2. Classify actual sends as autonomous client, authenticated human, or operational notification. Include scheduled/manual-triggered automated followups; exclude actual human responses from an indiscriminate bot gate.
3. Guard before generation and at every actual autonomous send boundary, including greetings, FAQ, media fallback, LLM failure, waiting acknowledgements and queued followups. Unknown/corrupt stop/binding reads fail closed for autonomous content.
4. Serialize handoff commitment and send admission across processes; a `SELECT` followed by awaited network I/O has a race. Use an explicit lock/admission design and bounded network handling. Specify the linearization point and uncertain provider outcomes: a message already admitted/in flight cannot be recalled by a later database commit.
5. A committed latch survives restart, policy expiry, disabling an enrollment flag and legacy intercept TTL/manual return-to-bot. Default-OFF activation alone is not durable suppression of an existing latch.
6. Prove these behaviors using mocked adapters and actual competing transactions, not live sends. A single isolated helper test or route demo cannot establish multi-channel H04.

This integration is deliberately outside the accepted core and does not block its useful local implementation.
