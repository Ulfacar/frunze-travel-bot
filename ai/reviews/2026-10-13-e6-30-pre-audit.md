## Smallest coherent first slice

1. Add an explicit native **open preliminary case** operation using a chosen existing commercial product/published workflow and current authorized owner. Bind contact/route/owner on the server, using the existing conversation identity and ownership checks. Do not silently seed/approve an inbound product or copy a tourist's identity from the payer. Opening does not mean signature, sale, personal-data consent, approved visa type or an invoice. Expose the existing applicant/intake tools through their current access gates.
2. Add an explicit **record already signed contract on this case** operation, either a separate helper or a carefully isolated optional `existing_case_id` branch. It must preserve that case, applicant, application, profile, interview, history and quote identity. Legacy `sign_contract(...)` calls without a target retain their existing new-case behavior. The new path takes/rechecks the expected case revision and current target ownership; posted contact/product/owner must not retarget it.
3. Offer a visible route from the preliminary case to its existing applicant/profile/interview/assessment/application/quote screens and back to its own signature form. The first slice can demonstrate an internal estimate before recording a real signature, but cannot label it an approved client offer. No new automatic legal/financial gate may be inferred merely from an estimate or `qualified_recorded_plan`.

The commercial `Product` and visa document-package product are distinct. `ServiceCase.product_id`, `contact_id` and pinned `workflow_version_id` are immutable. Choosing a commercial visa product at opening does not establish TS/B/EP; never rewrite it later to simulate visa selection. New workflow publication must not repin an existing preliminary case. On first signature, conservatively require the chosen product still active; an exact historical retry must remain distinguishable from a new signature. Existing archived-product replay behavior stays intact.

### Idempotency and journal constraints

The existing single `ServiceCase.idempotency_key` can be a case-lifetime creation key, but **a key alone is insufficient to prove two different operations**. The old `_assert_same_contract` compares current owner/amount fields; these may change after reassignment or a financial correction. Do not reuse that comparison as the entire new retained-open/sign receipt contract.

A new table is not inherently necessary. Existing append-only `ServiceEvent` can retain versioned normalized open/sign commands and their result identities, provided the implementation proves all of the following:

- Global creation-key uniqueness resolves simultaneous opens; normalize and compare the immutable original request, including subject, product, original workflow/owner/request and actor. A collision must not reveal another case. Do not overwrite/clear the opening key on signature and lose delayed-open recovery.
- A case lock plus revision check serializes first signature. Two distinct signatures on one case cannot both succeed. Store a separate exact signature operation identity/command in the signed event; bind the native envelope to action, target case, actor and session.
- A committed duplicate is recognized before a stale-revision/new-authority check that would otherwise mislabel it as a new request; current access is still required. Late duplicates after new workflow publication, quote revision, reassignment or price correction neither add tasks/events nor revert current fields. Return the retained historical operation and current state distinctly if needed.
- Do not weaken legacy request semantics or reinterpret old `case_opened`/`contract_signed` payloads. New metadata must be versioned. In particular, preserve the existing signed event's top-level `reference`, `amount`, `currency`, `revision` consumed by other services.
- Signature fields, event, operation evidence and initial task must commit atomically. Rollback of the helper must preserve unrelated caller writes; cancellation/failure must not leave a half-signed case. Lost HTTP response uses the original signed form/key, not a new contract.

`_create_initial_tasks:189` selects the **current** case stage, not necessarily the workflow's first stage. For the smallest slice, prevent an unsigned commercial case from being advanced by the new flow and require its original first stage for first signature. Otherwise deliberately define and test the task selection; silently missing `first_task` is unacceptable. The separate KG process stage must not be confused with the commercial stage.

### Money and authority boundaries

Retain `_contract_amount:337`: positive finite Decimal/string, at most two fractional digits, range checks and currency; or NULL amount with a nonempty mutually exclusive unknown reason. Unknown is not zero. Preserve partial payments, mixed-currency/explicit-FX handling, refunds and manager/admin permissions. A saved quote never overwrites agreed contract money, creates a payment, or treats a refund obligation as paid.

The existing money API does not itself require `signed_at`; the case reader suppresses the balance until signed. The case panel also has money controls independent of signature. This is a concrete integration hazard when unsigned cases become reachable: do not claim opening creates a payable balance or automatically use pre-existing money as full payment. Preserve explicit historical money facts and test their behavior; do not invent a blanket new payment policy in this slice. If a new UI chooses not to expose an action yet, that is not evidence that the old domain API forbids it.

Recording an already signed contract is an explicit human assertion of a commercial fact, not source-law approval. Keep it compatible with an unknown agreed price, as currently supported. Do not require a new automatic eligibility result merely to record an existing signed fact. Conversely, a **new source-derived proposal** needs its own current authority and exact dependency checks; recording a contract cannot approve that proposal. Signature/offer acceptance must not manufacture interview consent or undo withdrawal.

## Final design follow-up: separate open receipt

After the source assessment, read the new task `ai/tasks/2026-10-13-e6-30-precontract-case.md`. Its bounded target is consistent with this pre-audit. The proposed **separate immutable open-receipt table**, unique on open request key and case ID, is a sensible small alternative and preferable to overloading the old signing column if it keeps `ServiceCase.idempotency_key` dedicated to legacy signing semantics. This is a technical implementation choice, not a new business approval requirement.

Make open and sign explicit operation namespaces. Open receipt, new case and `case_opened` must commit together; enforce FK/uniqueness in PostgreSQL and immutable ORM/bulk-write guards consistent with the project. Keep a normalized versioned command, original server-derived subject/owner/workflow, actor and result identity so late retries don't compare mutable case fields. Do not silently invent receipts for legacy rows. The new table holds one opening receipt per new case, not another unbounded per-case journal.

Keep signing evidence versioned in the compatible `contract_signed` event and serialize it on the case. A partial/unique index over shared legacy `ServiceEvent` JSON is possible, but has a broader compatibility surface and is not necessary merely to avoid a small dedicated table. If the dedicated-table design is selected, add an additive migration, duplicate/rollback constraints, preservation of old signed and unsigned rows, and explicit populated downgrade/recovery behavior. None of this removes the independent review/audit or actual PostgreSQL acceptance gates.
