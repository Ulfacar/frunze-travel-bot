   `KG.VR.GENERAL` returned `answered` with `Countries (ISO): all_others`, without
   defining the exclusion set. `_answer` now withholds this case with
   `catchall_scope_requires_specialist`. Independent original-case retest PASS.

   a generic title returned only `Days: 17`; country and kind were left implicit in
   the technical ID. `_answer` now explicitly states registration exemption/term
   and the schema-validated ISO country suffix. Independent retest PASS.
3. **AC7, incomplete counting basis.** An imported, reviewed and activated numeric
   rule `{value:45, unit:days}` without anchor/boundary returned `answered` on r2.
   Numeric day rules now require anchor and inclusive fields or specialist fallback.
   Independent original-case retest PASS.
4. **AC6, external resources.** r2 inherited unpkg JavaScript and Google font links
   from `base.html`. Optional base blocks now let this page suppress those resources.
   Actual browser probes assert zero third-party request attempts and no preconnect
   links on the answer screen. PASS.
