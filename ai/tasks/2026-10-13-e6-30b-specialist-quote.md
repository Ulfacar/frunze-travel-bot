## Boundaries and design

- Separate empty-by-default specialist delegation. Publication, collection and
  calculation permissions do not imply permission to make this decision. Existing
  full-admin/current-configured-identity gate remains; no manager rights expansion.
- Deterministic source index with original PDF/full-text archive/page/block hashes
  and source section identities. §19 product clauses are not present in the208
  executable units: their separate explicit clause-review authority is necessary.
  Source preservation is not approval; relevant existing publication availability
  and unresolved flags still apply. Never silently promote real sources.
- One bounded immutable decision journal with strict versioned command/result,
  subject, policy, source and fact pins; per-applicant revision/request uniqueness,
  stable historical semantics and reserved count/byte capacity for withdrawal.
  A quote action in this journal binds the existing quote; no second binding table.
- Source-indexed capability catalogue, initially only implemented narrow B outcome
  enabled by synthetic tests. Do not claim every SOP is implemented from enum names.
  All applicable material fields must be mapped or explicitly unresolved.
- Separate specialist profile/interview reconciliation, preserving the original
  immutable interview header and current ACK meaning. Later profile/history can
  be explicitly reconciled without creating another person/interview. Privacy
  holds, missing consent, unknown/contradictory material facts and unmapped branches
  cannot be overridden. Supported risk handoff is responsibility, not clearance.
- Existing E628 NO_VISA behavior and v1 standalone quote schemas/replay remain
  unchanged. Extract only a private same-session quote save/preview seam; preserve
  public APIs and calculator semantics. Do not add fields to exact v1 commands.
- Lock existing case/application/applicant/publication parents in established
  order, sample time after locks, recheck all authority/fact/recipe heads through
  commit. Quote and link must roll back together. No external provider in this
  transaction, no independent engine-owning save after a stale decision read.
- Internal planning, arithmetic completeness and current authority are distinct.
  Pending document collection may precede an internal estimate; it never records
  package acceptance, quote delivery, client acceptance, signature, payment,
  lawful stay/work permission or official approval. Existing money is untouched.
